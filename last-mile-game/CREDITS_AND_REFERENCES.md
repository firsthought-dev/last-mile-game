## 🎮 2. Gameplay & Design Inspirations

- **Slow Roads ([slowroads.io](https://slowroads.io)) by anslo:**
  - Procedural infinite driving serenity, minimalist aesthetic HUD, and procedural horizon curves.
- **Crazy Taxi (SEGA):**
  - High-momentum urban express delivery loop, countdown urgency, and combo multiplier streaks.
- **Indian Last-Mile Logistics & Highway Culture:**
  - The iconic Mumbai Dabbawalas (world-renowned precision lunchbox delivery network).
  - Indian highway goods carriers, Tata Ace "Chhota Hathi" mini-trucks, and auto-rickshaws.
  - Roadside Dhabas, Chai Tapris (cutting chai stalls), local Kirana general stores, and NH highway milestones.

## 🎨 3. 3D Models & Visual Assets

- **Kenney Car Kit ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)):**
  - Low-poly CC0 (Public Domain) 3D vehicles: Sports Sedan, Light Commercial Pickup Truck, and Delivery Van.
- **Procedural In-Engine 3D Architecture:**
  - Handcrafted procedural Three.js geometry generators for Indian Havelis, Wadas, Kirana shops, Chai Tapris, bus stops, repair bays, streetlamps, milestones, and city landmarks (Gateway of India, India Gate, Howrah Bridge, Shaniwar Wada, Vidhana Soudha).
- **Procedural Animated Characters:**
  - Low-poly animated pedestrian rig (dhoti/kurta, saree, jeans) and animals (Indian humpback cows, stray dogs, village goats).
