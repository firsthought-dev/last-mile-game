# 2026-10-29

- The in-game HUD now scales with the screen and keeps every panel in its own place, so the order card, speed, WANTED stars, road map and controls no longer overlap on any phone, tablet or desktop screen, in portrait or landscape; the road and the rider stay clear. See [[2026-10-28 - Comic Callouts In Game]].

- Delivery and police flow made more robust: a failure in the comic layer can no longer stall a drop.

- Throws from too far away no longer cost a spare parcel, and the game tells you how far the drop is. Running out of spare parcels no longer ends the shift with a popup.

- Pedestrian speech bubbles for red-light jumps show again. The Medium graphics setting now runs fewer cars and pedestrians than High.
