# 2026-09-22 - B91 Delivery Cycle Smooth Ride and Dynamics Overhaul

## Summary
The delivery bicycle was jerky; it now rides smoothly.
- **Steering no longer kills speed**: turning slows the bike a little instead of stalling it.
- **Smoother steering**: turns ease in instead of snapping, and the bike no longer spins out.
- **Natural pedaling**: a realistic pedaling rhythm replaces the fast vibration, and the bike glides when you stop pedaling.
- **Softer suspension**: bumps no longer rattle the bike.
- **Bicycle camera**: sits closer and lower, and follows more steadily.
