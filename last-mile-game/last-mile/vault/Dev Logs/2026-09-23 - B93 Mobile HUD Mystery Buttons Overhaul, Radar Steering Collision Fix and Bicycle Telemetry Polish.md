# 2026-09-23 — B93 Clearer Mobile HUD Buttons, Radar/Steering Overlap & Bicycle Gear Display

## Summary
- **Labeled buttons**: players couldn't tell what the five tiny emoji buttons did. They're replaced by 🤖 AUTO and ⚙️ TOOLS. TOOLS opens a drawer with labeled options: Camera View, Recenter Car, Photo Mode and Horn/Bell.
- **Radar no longer covered**: the steering ring used to sit over the minimap. It's now hidden until you touch the screen and appears under your thumb, and the radar sits above the steering area.
- **Bicycle gear display**: shows PEDAL or CRUISE on the bicycle instead of DRIVE.
- **DROP button**: centered above the pedals with more space, so it isn't hit by accident while braking.
