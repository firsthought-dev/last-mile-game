# 2026-10-05 — B136-B138 Playtest Fixes

Three fixes from an external playtest.

- **Parcel throws (B136):** a throw now only delivers when the drop is ahead of you and within reach. Throwing after you have passed the drop, or from far away, is a miss like any other.
- **Curve warning signs (B137):** the yellow chevron signs now stand on the outside of the bend and point the way the road turns, as on real roads.
- **Police wanted stars (B138):** clearer. The first time you earn a star in a save, a short note explains what earns stars and that 5 stars means you are caught. The star meter now reads "5★ = CAUGHT", and the settle-up box tells you in plain words why you were stopped.

Part of the ongoing polish pass; see [[Projects/Last-Mile Game]].
