# 2026-09-22 - B85 Vehicle Progression Economy & Mobile Drag Steer

## Summary
- **Career economy**: players start with the delivery bicycle and earn money to unlock the Muscle Coupe. The Dispatch Hub shows how much more is needed.
- **Unlock sheet**: shows the vehicle's stats and the cost before you confirm.
- **Vehicle cards**: illustrated previews of each vehicle.
- **Drag to steer**: on mobile, the bicycle steers by dragging, with haptic feedback.
- **Easy mode**: houses sit further back from the road.
- **Android**: updated to the Android API level Google Play requires.
