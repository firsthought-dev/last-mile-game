# 2026-09-22 - B92 Locked Vehicles Stay Locked After Reload

## Summary
Fixed an exploit: reloading the page and changing the visual style let players drive the locked Muscle Coupe for free.
- The game now remembers which vehicle you picked and checks it against what you've unlocked, every time a drive starts.
- The in-game vehicle switcher greys out locked vehicles and refuses them.
- The delivery card was also tightened on narrow phones.
