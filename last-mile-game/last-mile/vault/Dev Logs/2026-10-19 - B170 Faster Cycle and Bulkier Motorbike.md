# B170: Faster cycle, bulkier motorbike

**Date:** 2026-10-19

Reviewers found the delivery cycle too slow, so it now starts faster and tops out higher while staying below the motorbike. The motorbike was rebuilt to look like a real commuter bike (bigger tank, engine, mudguards, shocks, exhaust, wider tyres), and the side stand that stuck out was removed.
