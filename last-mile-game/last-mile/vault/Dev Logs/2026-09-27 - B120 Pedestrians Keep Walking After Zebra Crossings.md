# 2026-09-27 — B120 Pedestrians Keep Walking After Zebra Crossings
- **Fixed:** pedestrians at zebra crossings no longer walk across and straight back over and over. They wait for the red light, cross once and carry on with their walk.
- **Fixed:** after crossing, pedestrians no longer stand idle on the far sidewalk. They turn left or right, or head to a nearby house (and go inside), a tea stall, a cycle repair shop or a motor garage.
- **Improved:** roadside places, motor garages especially, now get regular visitors instead of standing empty.
- Follows up [[2026-09-23]] roadside life (B104), which covered sidewalk walkers but not the pedestrians placed at zebra crossings.
