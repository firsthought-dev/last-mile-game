# 2026-10-05 — B140–B143 Difficulty, Assist Bonus and HUD Tidy

Changes from an external playtest and the follow-up design decisions.

- **Spare parcels (B140):** each shift starts with three spare parcels, shown in the delivery capsule. A throw that can't reach a drop uses one. Missing with none left ends the shift ("Out of parcels"): the streak resets, and money already earned stays in your account.
- **Barrier hits cost speed (B140):** hitting a barrier now slows you noticeably, and scraping along it keeps slowing you.
- **No-assist bonus (B141):** Auto Steer and Auto Drive stay, and deliveries made without either pay 10% more. The delivery card shows the bonus.
- **Cleaner play screen (B142):** the audio and FPS indicators left the play screen. Sound is still on the dock and in Settings, and an FPS counter can be turned on in Settings › Display & Sound. Camera and time-of-day stay on screen.
- **Shift earnings fixed (B143):** shift totals no longer include money that wasn't earned, and the "double your shift" reward only counts the current shift.

Part of the ongoing polish pass; see [[Projects/Last-Mile Game]].
