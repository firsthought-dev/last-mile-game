# 2026-10-19 — B144 Sidewalk Pedestrians No Longer Count as Hits

Found while playing: riding along the edge of the road, next to people walking on the sidewalk, could earn wanted stars for "hitting a pedestrian" even though nobody was touched.

- People on the sidewalk now only count as hit if your vehicle has actually ridden up onto the sidewalk (for example at a driveway).
- Hits are judged from the real size of each vehicle, so close passes on the road no longer count as collisions.
- Pedestrians crossing the road can still be hit, and still earn stars.
