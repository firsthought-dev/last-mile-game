# 2026-09-23 - B107 Guardrails Aligned with the Sidewalk

## Summary
Players noticed guardrails and fences that weren't lined up with the sidewalk and that floated in places.
- Guardrails, stone walls and wood fences now run along the sidewalk's outer edge, with no gap between the sidewalk and the barrier.
- Posts and walls sit in the ground on crests, dips and banked bends, with no daylight underneath.
- Far down the road, barriers are built the same way.
- Street lamps sit on the ground in the same way.
