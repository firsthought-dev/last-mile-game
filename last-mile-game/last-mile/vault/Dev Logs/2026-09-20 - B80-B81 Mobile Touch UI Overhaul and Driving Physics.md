# 2026-09-20 - B80/B81 Mobile Touch UI Overhaul & Driving Physics

## Summary
- **Mobile HUD without overlaps**: steering sits bottom-left, pedals and DROP bottom-right, the delivery card top-left and speed/gear top-right. Checked on iPhone 16, Galaxy A55 and Pixel 9.
- **Driving feel**: gears have their own pull, the car dips under braking and squats when accelerating, and the bicycle leans into turns.
- **Portrait mode**: the camera widens its view when the phone is held upright.
- **Deliveries**: all made from the vehicle with Express Drops; the on-foot mode was removed.
