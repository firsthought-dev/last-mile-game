# 2026-10-25 - Crisp Style Removed

Part of the Mumbai-Pune city direction, on its own branch. Not merged. Bug ID: B209.

## What changed for players
- **Cinematic is now the only look.** The Crisp style is gone.
- Players who had picked Crisp (or one of the older styles) move to Cinematic automatically the next time they open the game.
- The "Select visual style" section is gone from the start menu, so the menu is simpler: pick a vehicle and drive.
- Cinematic itself looks the same as before.

## Why
Crisp was costly to draw, and its outlines did not always match the trees and buildings. One look is simpler to keep sharp and leaves more room on phones for the city work coming next.

## Status
- Desktop and phone-sized checks pass in both graphics quality tiers.
- Not yet checked on real phones.
